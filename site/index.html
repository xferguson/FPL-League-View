What the views mean

League average
The mean score of the teams in this league each gameweek.
FPL average
The official average score across every FPL manager that gameweek.
% Lg / % FPL
The same scores expressed as a percentage of that baseline. 100% is level with it, 120% is a fifth better. On the cumulative view this is the season-to-date ratio, which settles down as the weeks add up; per gameweek it swings much harder.

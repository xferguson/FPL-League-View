What the baselines mean

League average
The mean score of the teams in this league each gameweek.
FPL average
The official average score across every FPL manager that gameweek.
Index
A synthetic baseline: the mean score of every player who featured that gameweek, times eleven — roughly what a team of eleven completely average performers would have scored.
